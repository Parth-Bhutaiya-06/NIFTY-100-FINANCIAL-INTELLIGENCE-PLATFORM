NIFTY 100 FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM
Internship Expansion - what was updated and where
==========================================================================
Title: Explainable Financial Analytics, Peer Benchmarking and Automated Reporting

The existing project was extended, not replaced. Nothing in the original
ETL, KPI engine, API, tearsheets or existing dashboard pages was rewritten.
Only 3 existing files were edited (listed in section B).


A. NEW FILES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

MODULE 1 - DATA QUALITY MONITORING
  src/quality/__init__.py
  src/quality/periods.py
      One shared parser for period labels ("Mar 2024", "Mar-24", "2024",
      "TTM", "2024.5", "Mar 2016 9m"). Collapses duplicate label variants,
      detects each company's fiscal-year-end month, drops off-cycle rows
      (e.g. "Sep 2024" half-year rows beside "Mar" annual rows).
  src/quality/data_quality.py
      DataQualityMonitor. Four check families, each finding has a severity
      (error / warning / info):
        - missing values (+ companies missing from a table)
        - duplicate records (identical vs conflicting, label variants)
        - inconsistent periods (bad labels, TTM, off-cycle, gaps, tables
          ending in different years)
        - invalid metrics (balance sheet not balancing, negative sales,
          implausible ROE, cash flow not adding up, orphan company ids,
          bad stock prices, etc.)
      Also: summarize(), company_reliability() (High/Medium/Low),
      company_warnings() (text lines used in PDFs).
      Run:  python -m src.quality.data_quality
  output/data_quality_report.csv      (generated findings, 1,314 rows)

MODULE 2 - EXPLAINABLE FINANCIAL ANALYTICS
  src/analytics/financial_metrics.py
      Latest-full-year indicators computed from the raw statements:
      ROE, net margin, operating margin, debt-to-equity, interest coverage,
      revenue CAGR, profit CAGR, CFO/PAT, asset turnover, free cash flow.
      Impossible values are excluded with a written reason, never hidden.
  src/analytics/health_score.py
      Documented 0-100 health score. 8 metrics, 4 pillars (Profitability 30,
      Leverage 25, Growth 25, Cash quality 20). Straight-line points between
      two thresholds, weights re-scaled when data is missing, coverage
      reported, score withheld below 50% coverage. Banks/NBFCs/insurers are
      not scored on leverage-type metrics. Every result carries a component
      table (value, points, weight, contribution) that sums exactly to the
      score, plus strengths, concerns and caveats. Grades A-E.
  output/health_scores.csv            (league table, 92 companies)

MODULE 3 - PEER BENCHMARKING
  src/analytics/benchmarking.py
      Peer set = curated group from the peer_groups table (min 3 members),
      else broad sector. Per metric: company value, peer median (excluding
      the company), difference, percentile (direction-aware), rank, verdict.
      Also sector_summary().
  output/sector_benchmark.csv

  src/analytics/platform_data.py
      Loads the database once and computes issues, metrics, scores and
      league table. Used by the dashboard, the PDFs and the tests so every
      screen and PDF shows the same numbers.

C. WHAT THE DATA CHECK FOUND (confirmed from the repository's database)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Available: 92 companies, annual P&L / balance sheet / cash flow
  (about FY2013-FY2024), 5,520 price rows, market cap 2019-2024, 11 curated
  peer groups (56 companies), 10 broad sectors.
  Data problems the monitor reports (and the analytics work around):
    - 26 rows of statements belong to ids not in the company master
      (WIPRO, VEDL, ZOMATO, ULTRACEMCO in some tables ...)
    - mixed period labels; TTM rows; "Sep 2024" half-year rows
    - duplicate rows per company-year (identical and conflicting)
    - impossible stored ratios (BEL ROE > 4,000%)
    - expenses / operating profit that break the accounting identity
    - only 56 of 92 companies have a curated peer group; the rest are
      benchmarked against their broad sector
  Not available, so NOT used: sub-annual financials, forecasts, any paid
  AI. The score does not use stock prices and does not predict prices.
